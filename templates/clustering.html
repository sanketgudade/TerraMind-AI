How the AI clustering works

A viva-friendly explanation of the algorithm and the current parameters.

01 · Density-based grouping

DBSCAN groups points that are geographically close and sufficiently dense. It does not require the number of clusters to be specified in advance.

02 · Neighborhood radius (eps)

The backend currently uses a 5 km neighborhood radius. The geographic coordinates are converted to radians so DBSCAN can use the haversine distance metric.

03 · Minimum samples

The backend uses at least 10 points in a neighborhood to identify a core point. Other points may join a cluster as border points. Points that do not join any cluster are labelled as noise.
